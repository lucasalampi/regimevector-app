EDITION 130
Stocks were mixed as chipmakers gave back part of Monday's surge: the S&P 500 fell 0.5% and the Nasdaq 1.5%, with Nvidia down 5.9%, while the Dow rose 0.9% back above 50,000 and small caps gained 0.6%. Health care and consumer staples led. The dollar weakened, Treasury yields were steady and gold fell 2.4%. Oil rose after new attacks on ships, with WTI at $82.05 and Brent at $91.40. Our reading stays DISINFLATION / HAWKISH PIVOT; two of four conditions are met.Tuesday turned Monday's one-stock rally into a rotation: chips fell, health care and staples rose, and the equal-weight index beat the cap-weighted one by a point. That is healthier breadth but a weaker index, and the S&P now sits right at the line where we would call the trend broken. Rates did not move and oil rose on new strait attacks. Wednesday's inflation report matters more than either.
